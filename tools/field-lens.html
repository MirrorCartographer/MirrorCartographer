Field Lens.

A fact is a point. A claim is a shape drawn between points. The space between them is where the work begins.

Add observations, choose a relationship, and inspect the claim that emerges. This instrument does not calculate truth. It makes provenance, missing evidence, and the difference between observation and interpretation visible.
