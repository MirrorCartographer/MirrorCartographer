## 5. Key distinctions to preserve

1. **Resistance versus tolerance:** resistance reduces infection or threat; tolerance reduces damage while the threat persists. The mosquito-cell paper is particularly relevant to survival/tolerance, not simply viral elimination.
2. **IAP versus IAAP:** inhibitor-of-apoptosis protein versus iodoaryl azidoprazosin probe. Similar letters do not mean same molecule or mechanism.
3. **Binding inhibition versus transport inhibition:** a compound reducing probe binding/photolabeling does not, alone, prove a specific binding-site mechanism or clinically useful P-gp inhibition.
4. **Tracer uptake versus transporter specificity:** sestamibi retention can be influenced by multiple biological and technical factors; it is not a perfect one-to-one measure of P-gp.
5. **Fluorescence intensity versus state:** intensity is not synonymous with activity, health, viral burden, or causal mechanism.
6. **Temporal order versus causation:** one signal preceding another is a clue, not proof that the first caused the second.
7. **Prediction versus explanation:** an AI model may predict outcomes without revealing the mechanism.
8. **Structural analogy versus shared mechanism:** similarities between molecular, cellular, social, and AI networks generate questions; they do not establish equivalence.
9. **Scientific evidence versus interpretive meaning:** empirical biology, social science, philosophy, and religion have different objects and validation practices.
10. **Cell-line results versus whole-organism results:** C6/36 cell findings must not be generalized automatically to all mosquitoes, viruses, or natural transmission contexts.

## 9. Conversation-level conclusion

The most defensible shared framework is not “everything is the same kind of computer.” It is:

**Systems have internal states; interactions change those states; measurements expose only selected aspects of them; models infer hidden relationships; interventions test those inferences; and communities of observers decide how to interpret and use the results.**

That framework supports meaningful cross-domain inquiry while keeping biological mechanisms, computational abstractions, social dynamics, and religious meaning distinct.
