# Research method: from fragment to warranted conclusion

## Purpose
This protocol turns Mirror Cartographer's research ambitions into reviewable work. It organizes evidence and designs tests; it does not claim a validated scientific discovery or improved clinical or benefits outcome.

## Four research lanes
1. **Biomedical mechanisms.** Specify disease, biological system, treatment, population, and outcome. Distinguish association, mechanism, model-system result, and clinical benefit. Map candidate mechanisms and interactions, then state what experiment could discriminate among explanations.
2. **Veteran welfare and access.** Model the path from nominal eligibility to realized outcome: awareness → eligibility interpretation → application/referral → documentation → scheduling/provider availability → service received → follow-up/outcome. Record friction, handoffs, time, costs, and attrition. An entitlement is not proof a particular person can access it.
3. **Evidence and knowledge architecture.** Link each claim to source records and a dated interpretation. Preserve source identity, supporting passage, methods, population, limitations, transformations, dissent, and revision history. Missing is not negative; repeated is not independent; correlation is not causation.
4. **Human-centered AI and creative technology.** Treat interface interactions as researchable interventions. Define user task, accessibility conditions, comparison, outcome measure, adverse effects, and stopping criteria. Delight and metaphor are design hypotheses—not evidence of efficacy.

## Claim lifecycle
unreviewed → source-checked → qualified → tested / corroborated / contested → revised or retired.

These are workflow states, not a universal scientific evidence hierarchy. A claim can be well-sourced but indirect; replicated but narrow; plausible but untested. Record source quality and claim scope separately.

## Required claim record
- Stable claim ID and concise proposition
- Domain, population/system, context, time window, and outcome
- Claim type: observation, source-reported finding, synthesis, inference, hypothesis, design proposal, or value judgment
- Source identifiers and exact supporting locations
- Source type, method, sample/context, publication date, and access date
- Evidence direction: supports, contradicts, qualifies, or does not address
- Confidence rationale and important limitations
- Alternative explanations and missing data
- Falsification/disconfirmation condition
- Next discriminating observation or experiment
- Author, reviewer, revision date, and change log
- Privacy/licensing review and permissible reuse

## Research quality gates
A claim cannot be promoted merely because a source sounds authoritative or several pages repeat it. Review:
- **Provenance:** Can a reader recover the source and supporting location?
- **Fit:** Does the source study the same population, intervention, mechanism, and outcome?
- **Independence:** Are sources independent or repeating one underlying study?
- **Uncertainty:** Are limitations, missingness, disagreement, and alternatives visible?
- **Causal restraint:** Does the design justify causal language?
- **Actionability:** Is the proposed next step proportionate to the evidence?
- **Privacy and ethics:** Is material public-safe, consented where needed, and appropriately licensed?
- **Reproducibility:** Are search date, method, inclusion logic, transformations, and validation instructions recorded?

## Domain-specific boundaries
### Biomedical
Do not infer a treatment recommendation from a mechanistic review. Separate cell culture, animal, observational human, and interventional clinical evidence. State disease and treatment scope. A pathway that explains resistance in one cancer is not a general explanation for all persistent disease. Experiments need controls, measurable outcomes, appropriate replication, and independent scientific review before translational claims.

### Veteran services
Separate statute/policy, agency guidance, administrative performance data, surveys, qualitative accounts, and individual experience. Qualitative reports identify mechanisms and failure modes but do not establish population prevalence. Policies change: record the date checked and link to current official sources before advising on eligibility or deadlines. Never infer an individual's eligibility from general research.

### Knowledge systems
Use qualified links between claims and sources. Preserve raw observation separately from interpretation. Keep contradictions instead of averaging them away. Track changes to claim scope, evidence status, and decision consequences.

### Human-centered AI
A fluent explanation is not proof of accuracy. A user finding an interpretation resonant is not proof it is true. Test usability, comprehension, error detection, accessibility, user agency, privacy, and outcomes separately. Make opt-out and correction easy.

## How to use the initial evidence register
The first register is a bounded starting sample, not a systematic review. It contains sources that justify investigating specific mechanisms and access barriers. It does not establish every hypothesis listed. Re-run searches, document selection criteria, add contrary evidence, and have domain experts review before stronger claims are made.
