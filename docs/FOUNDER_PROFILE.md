## Professional summary

Charity Alessandra Sturgell is the founder and project lead of Mirror Cartographer, an independent research and software project exploring how human meaning, complex information, and AI-assisted work can remain inspectable across time, tools, and changing contexts. Her work spans human-centered AI interaction, symbolic mapping, provenance-aware continuity, evaluation design, creative coding, and evidence-oriented research.

She directs product framing, system requirements, research questions, interaction design, and iterative acceptance. She uses AI-assisted development and third-party tools to prototype and test interfaces, data structures, evaluation workflows, and provider integrations. Her professional emphasis is translating ambiguous, high-context problems into structured systems that can be inspected, challenged, and improved.
