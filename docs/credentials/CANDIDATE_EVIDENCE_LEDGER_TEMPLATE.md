## Required claim classes

- **Observed:** directly inspected in the cited artifact or test result.
- **Reported:** stated by a person or system; not independently confirmed.
- **Inferred:** reasoned from available evidence; alternatives remain possible.
- **Proposed:** a plan, design, recommendation or intended future state.
- **Unknown:** evidence is missing, inaccessible, conflicting or insufficient.

## Evidence handling

For every entry:
1. Link to the exact artifact, commit, test output or source.
2. Quote only the minimum text needed to identify the claim.
3. Record the date and version inspected.
4. Identify what would falsify or weaken the claim.
5. Do not turn absence of evidence into evidence of absence.
6. Record corrections without deleting the prior record.
