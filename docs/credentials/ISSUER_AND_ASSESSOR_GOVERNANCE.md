# Issuer and Independent Assessor Governance

## Issuer

The issuing entity for internal Mirror Cartographer credentials is the Mirror Cartographer project, represented by its project owner. The issuer must identify itself transparently and must not imply institutional accreditation, government recognition, university authority, or endorsement by 1EdTech, Microsoft, GitHub, IBM, or another third party.

## Founder recognition versus competency certification

The project owner may issue a founder recognition record for documented project-originating work. This is a recognition of contribution and project role, not an independently assessed competency credential.

The founder may not be the sole assessor of their own technical competency credential. The founder-recipient relationship must be disclosed in the record.

## Independent assessor requirements

An independent assessor must be a real, identifiable person with relevant subject-matter competence and no material role in creating the candidate's assessed work. The assessor must:

- provide their real name and professional profile or institutional affiliation, if any;
- disclose financial, personal, employment, authorship, supervisory and collaboration relationships with the candidate;
- declare any conflict of interest before seeing the assessment submission;
- accept the published rubric and assessment conditions;
- review the source evidence and candidate-performed practical task;
- provide dimension-level scores and written rationale;
- sign the assessment decision and permit verification of their role.

A simulated persona, AI agent, language model, project owner, or assistant cannot be represented as an independent human assessor.

## AI's permitted role

An AI assessment assistant may:
- organize evidence and identify gaps;
- map evidence to published criteria;
- generate practice tasks;
- check consistency and completeness;
- draft feedback for human review.

It may not independently validate its own generated work, impersonate an independent assessor, or sign a credential as a human reviewer.

## Independent review workflow

1. Candidate submits evidence ledger and task artifacts.
2. Program publishes the exact criteria and assessment version.
3. Independent assessor completes conflict disclosure.
4. Assessor conducts a practical assessment and records conditions.
5. Assessor scores each dimension and provides written rationale.
6. A second reviewer is required for Level 3/4 or where the first assessor has a potential conflict.
7. Issuer verifies the record, signs the credential, and publishes a revocation/correction endpoint.
8. Candidate receives the portable credential and verification instructions.
