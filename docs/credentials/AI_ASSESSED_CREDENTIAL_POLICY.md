# AI-Assessed Credential Policy

**Program:** Mirror Cartographer Credential Program  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective:** 2026-11-09  
**Issuer:** Mirror Cartographer project  
**Public contact:** MirrorCartographer@gmail.com

## What an AI-assessed credential means

Mirror Cartographer may use an AI system as the assessor of a project-based competency assessment. The credential is issued by the project, based on a disclosed AI evaluation of documented evidence and candidate responses.

The AI assessor is not represented as a human, independent professional, university, employer, vendor, regulator, or accrediting organization. The credential is not a professional license or an accredited qualification.

## Required disclosures

Every AI-assessed credential must show:
- “AI-assessed project credential” in its credential description.
- Issuer: Mirror Cartographer project.
- Assessor: AI system; exact model/version when available.
- Candidate relationship: project founder/owner, where applicable.
- Assessment date, rubric version, score, level, and criteria.
- Evidence URLs and a concise rationale for each scored dimension.
- Assessment limitations, including anything not directly demonstrated.
- A stable verification page and correction/revocation contact.

## Evidence and scoring rules

The assessor scores five dimensions from 0–4: conceptual understanding, evidence quality/traceability, practical execution, reasoning/trade-offs, and safety/accessibility/privacy/integrity. Each score requires a rationale and a direct evidence reference.

A missing artifact is not proof of competence. Project ownership is not proof of independent coding. AI-generated code is not proof that the candidate personally implemented it. Candidate-originated requirements, architecture choices, prioritization, testing direction, critique, and acceptance decisions may be credited when the record supports them.

Pass: at least 75/100, no dimension below 2, and no critical failure. Level 3 requires direct evidence of independent task completion. Level 4 requires advanced practical evidence and a second reviewer (human or separately configured AI review) whose identity and method are disclosed. Scores below threshold receive feedback, not a certificate.

## Credential lanes

1. **AI-assessed project credential** — available now under this policy, with full disclosure.
2. **Human-reviewed project credential** — optional additional review by a named human assessor.
3. **External certification** — only the external provider can award its own credential. Mirror Cartographer may prepare and map evidence but cannot issue vendor or academic certifications.

## LinkedIn representation

Use LinkedIn's Licenses & Certifications section only for a credential actually awarded. Use the exact credential title, Mirror Cartographer as issuing organization, actual issue date, and a stable public credential URL. Put “AI-assessed project credential; not accredited” in the credential description. Do not list an in-progress assessment as earned.

Suggested title pattern:
`[Competency] — AI-Assessed Project Credential (Level X)`

Suggested description:
`Project-issued, AI-assessed credential based on a documented portfolio and rubric. Assessor: [model/system]. Evidence, scoring criteria, limitations, and verification record: [URL]. Not an accredited qualification or professional license.`

## Corrections and verification

Send correction requests to MirrorCartographer@gmail.com with the credential ID and supporting evidence. Preserve prior versions and publish a correction/revocation notice where needed. Never expose private assessment material or personal data without consent.
