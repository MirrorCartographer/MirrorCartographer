# Portfolio case studies

**Founder / project lead:** Charity Alessandra Sturgell  
**Project:** Mirror Cartographer  
**Updated:** 2026-11-05

This page translates the work into inspectable case studies. Each case states the problem, the contribution, the artifact, the evidence, and the boundary. It is designed for technical reviewers and hiring teams.

## Case study 1 — Human-centered symbolic reflection system

**Problem.** Human experience is often nonlinear, embodied, metaphorical, and context-dependent. Conventional forms flatten that information into labels or isolated text fields.

**Contribution.** The project lead originated the symbolic self-mapping concept and directed a system using language, color, texture, spatial/body location, metaphor, and reflective prompts. AI assistance was used to structure specifications and implement prototypes.

**Inspectable artifacts.**
- [Private/public UI project](https://github.com/MirrorCartographer/mirror-cartographer-ui)
- [Research project](https://github.com/MirrorCartographer/MirrorCartographer)
- Recovered Canvas corpus: 38 documents and 129 snapshots (maintained separately from public repositories).

**Evidence boundary.** The recovered artifacts establish product design and prototype development. They do not establish clinical efficacy or validated therapeutic outcomes.

## Case study 2 — Provenance-aware continuity and system inspection

**Problem.** Long-running AI-assisted work loses context across sessions, tools, versions, and providers. A conclusion may become detached from the source that justified it.

**Contribution.** The project lead directed continuity, provenance, contradiction preservation, and inspectability requirements. AI-assisted implementation produced memory indexes, structured records, source-attributed retrieval, and project inspection tools.

**Inspectable evidence.** Private Repository C contains the implementation and tests; its public-facing completion map is summarized in the research repository. The private source remains private.

**Evidence boundary.** Source-level retrieval and integrity tests exist. Full end-to-end graph semantics, encrypted cross-device storage, and every historical session recovery path are not yet complete.

## Case study 3 — Human judgment and evaluation design

**Problem.** Agreement, confidence, and correctness are often treated as interchangeable even though a unanimous panel can share a bias.

**Contribution.** The project lead directed an evaluation lab that separates agreement, accuracy, confidence, evidence quality, and decision quality. AI assistance supported rubric and experiment implementation.

**Inspectable evidence.** The private Human Judgment Lab contains an evaluation program, six-dimension rubric, synthetic dataset, Consensus Trap simulation, run export, and deterministic tests.

**Evidence boundary.** The simulation is reproducible under its assumptions. It is not a field study or validated claim about real human panels.

## Case study 4 — Procedural creative systems

**Problem.** Creative tools can make generative behavior inspectable and remixable without requiring a proprietary image-generation backend.

**Contribution.** The project lead directed a procedural visual/audio/motion portal and independent image-organism experiments. Implementation uses browser APIs, seeded geometry, SVG, CSS, and local artifact storage.

**Inspectable evidence.** The reconstructed [Public Starter v1](../agents/mirror-cartographer-public-starter-v1/README.md) provides model-agnostic system instructions, onboarding, a symbolic-state JSON schema, privacy and safety rules, and acceptance tests. The recovered [Creation Portal v1 source](../evidence/historical/creation-portal-v1.html) is a standalone browser prototype with a localStorage artifact archive; its inline JavaScript passed syntax validation. The [Implementation Pack v1](../evidence/historical/implementation-pack-v1.md) and [Access Implementation Pack v2](../evidence/historical/access-implementation-pack-v2.md) preserve the product instructions, symbolic ontology, state contract, reflection logic, user flow, safety constraints, and developer handoff. Newer public UI artifacts include a local-first execution hub.

**Evidence boundary.** These are procedural generators and interactive prototypes, not evidence that a trained image model or external image-generation infrastructure was created.

## Case study 5 — Evidence-aware research and public-interest systems

**Problem.** Complex biomedical, veteran-service, and human-technology problems require context, time, provenance, missingness, and explicit uncertainty.

**Contribution.** The project lead set the research questions and designed cross-domain mapping frameworks. AI assistance supported synthesis, documentation, and prototype creation.

**Inspectable evidence.** Research expeditions, the Observatory, phenotype-trajectory documentation, and private domain-specific tools.

**Evidence boundary.** Research maps and prototypes do not establish medical diagnoses, treatment efficacy, policy impact, or deployed service outcomes.
