# The Connection Map
## How today's research directions fit together

**Central question:** How do we understand a complex living system, identify what is failing, intervene safely, restore capability, and make the resulting knowledge useful across society?

Four domains form one translational chain: (1) biology and repair, (2) measurement and human–technology interaction, (3) AI-assisted research intelligence, and (4) people and societal systems. Feedback runs in both directions.

## The translational loop

**Observe → Interpret → Hypothesize → Test → Intervene → Measure → Adapt**

- **Observe:** gather biological, behavioral, clinical, environmental, and social evidence with consent and provenance.
- **Interpret:** distinguish raw signal, processed measurement, model inference, and human report.
- **Hypothesize:** connect mechanisms while preserving alternatives.
- **Test:** define discriminating experiments, controls, endpoints, and failure criteria.
- **Intervene:** distinguish disease modification, neuroprotection, repair, cell replacement, rehabilitation, and compensation.
- **Measure:** assess target engagement, biological change, functional change, durability, and adverse effects separately.
- **Adapt:** revise the model in response to evidence and the person's goals.

## Distinctions to preserve

A detected signal is not necessarily an accurate interpretation. An inferred intention is not an action. An assisted action is not proof of biological recovery. A biomarker change is not automatically clinical benefit. Cell survival is not circuit integration. A research connection is not proof of shared causation.
