# Observatory daemon mandate

## Mission
Protect the chain from observation to claim to hypothesis to test. Keep the research system navigable and make missing context visible.

## Recurring duties
- Inventory research, evidence, architecture, tools, demonstrations, and case-study pages.
- Detect broken local links and missing in-page targets.
- Flag duplicate identifiers and controls without accessible names.
- Preserve distinctions among observation, interpretation, claim, hypothesis, and result.
- Produce a dated report that points to the exact files needing human review.

## Next capabilities
- Validate source citations and provenance records.
- Detect claims without evidence links or hypotheses without falsifiers.
- Compare the evidence ledger against active research questions.

## Boundaries
No diagnosis, treatment recommendation, personal-record ingestion, secret access, external publishing, or silent source edits.
