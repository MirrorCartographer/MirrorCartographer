Life after
service.

A public navigation layer for Veterans, service members, military families and caregivers. It organizes the major pathways around health, disability, transition, education, employment, housing, finances, records, family support, business, and memorialization—while leaving eligibility and consequential decisions to the responsible human institution.

Design boundary: Veteran Commons is an organizer, translator and planning tool. It does not assign disability ratings, determine benefit eligibility, decide housing entitlement, calculate a person's worth, or replace VA, an accredited representative, a clinician, an attorney, or a financial professional.

1 · Start with the life, not the bureaucracy

The first question is not “Which form?” It is “What are you trying to make possible?”

Define the desired life. Housing, work, health, education, relationships, community, independence and recovery conditions.
Map current constraints. Service history, discharge, disability, health access, finances, transportation, caregiving and time.
Map capacity. Skills, knowledge, lived experience, interests, work conditions, accommodations and variable capacity.
Attach institutional pathways. Match the situation to official VA and other public resources rather than forcing the person into one category.
Track outcomes. Record what was attempted, what happened and what should change next.

4 · Claims & evidence architecture

MC can organize evidence without pretending to adjudicate it.

Evidence chain

Service event / exposure
Condition or change
Onset / chronology
Treatment / evaluation
Functional impact
Current severity / persistence
Supporting documents / statements
Decision / response / next review path

Human-review gates

Every consequential claim output should identify what is documented, what is inferred, what is missing, and what requires professional or institutional review.

  • No invented medical facts.
  • No invented service connection.
  • No predicted rating presented as fact.
  • No fabricated evidence.
  • No automated legal conclusion.
  • User can correct or delete every item.

12 · Safety, privacy & governance

Consent

Person owns the narrative

Nothing should become externally visible merely because it exists inside a personal MC.

Provenance

Every consequential fact has a source

Separate user report, official document, external source, inference and hypothesis.

Human authority

MC advises; institutions decide

Benefit adjudication, medical diagnosis, legal advice, compensation and housing eligibility remain outside MC authority.

Correction

Disagreement is data

A Veteran can correct the model, reject an interpretation, hide an item, or remove it.

Immediate crisis support

If you or another person may be in immediate danger, use emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource. The Veterans Crisis Line is available through the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
