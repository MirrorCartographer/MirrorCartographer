Independent Assessor Worksheet

A structured review form for a real human assessor. This page does not appoint an assessor, certify their independence, issue a credential, or transmit data. Completed records are downloaded locally as JSON for issuer review.

Independence rule: the candidate, project owner, AI assistant, or anyone materially involved in creating the assessed work cannot be represented as the independent human assessor. A qualified external reviewer must provide their real identity and disclose conflicts.
