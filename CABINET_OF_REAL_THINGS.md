# The Cabinet of Real Things
*What has already taken shape*

A useful obsession leaves artifacts. Not every artifact is finished, and not every proposal has been tested. This cabinet distinguishes the work that exists from the work it points toward.

## A working demonstration catalogue
[**Project 101: Mirror Cartographer Demonstrations**](demonstrations/DEMONSTRATION_CATALOG_101.md)

The catalogue gives the project a testable anatomy: question, why it matters, input, transformation, observable proof of value, and boundary. Its ideas include the Fragment Becomes a Field, the Hinge Finder, the Return Path, the Boredom Alarm, and the What Changed? Audit.

These are demonstration concepts in a catalogue; each title should not be mistaken for a fully implemented and validated demo. The important result is that the project has a repeatable grammar for turning a grand claim into something that can be inspected.

## A research artifact with a real structure
[**Phenotype Trajectory Packet**](research/cure_discovery/2026-07-06-phenotype-trajectory-packet.md)

This packet turns a broad biomedical idea into a more disciplined research object: observed feature, time course, context window, measurement modality, source boundary, ontology mapping, missingness, evidence strength, and a route to falsification.

It does not establish a cure or diagnose an individual. It demonstrates a more useful question: what must a longitudinal observation preserve before anyone can responsibly compare it with another?

## A small story with a complete arc
[**Mallow — Three-Minute Animatic screenplay**](Creative_Universe/03_Stories/Mallow_The_Slug/screenplay.md)

Mallow moves slowly while faster creatures rush past. The moon breaks into fragments in a pond. Mallow's trail catches the moonlight; rain, danger, a broken trail, and glowing droplets transform the journey. At dawn, the shining path remains.

Its final thought is simple: **slow is still a way through.**

The idea has a screenplay/beat sheet. Do not mistake that file alone for a completed animated film. Its emotional architecture already exists on the page.

## A standalone world with its own laws
[**The House That Forgets Gravity**](independent-creative/the-house-that-forgets-gravity/IDENTITY.md)

A room accepts a visitor's choice of gravity as physical law. Furniture, language, sound, and the house itself obey. Handmade theatrical geometry gives the changed rule a visible consequence.

This piece deliberately stands on its own. It does not need a dashboard, research metaphor, or project explanation to justify its existence. The world is the point.

## What these artifacts reveal together

They look different, but share a structural instinct:

- A fragment should become a field without losing its origin.
- A model should preserve change over time, not merely collect labels.
- A story can make a hidden structure felt.
- A physical rule becomes meaningful when the world responds to it.
- A claim should carry its boundary with it.

That is a result already visible in the work: **the project is developing a common method for preserving context while changing the form in which context can be explored.**

## How to use this cabinet

Choose one artifact and ask:

1. What can I actually inspect right now?
2. What is specified but not yet built?
3. What has been tested, and where is the test result recorded?
4. What would make this more complete without making it more generic?

Keep the difference between artifact, intention, and proof visible. That distinction is not a dampener on ambition; it is how ambition becomes usable.
