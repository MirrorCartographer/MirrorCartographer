Mirror Cartographer
Mirror Cartographer is a human-centered AI reflection system exploring symbolic emotional mapping,
psychological orientation, and meaningful interaction inside AI-mediated environments. The project
focuses on emotionally sustainable AI interaction rather than productivity optimization alone.
Core Areas
• Symbolic cognition
• Emotional mapping
• Reflective AI interaction
• Human-AI meaning systems
• Neurodivergent-accessible interfaces
• Immersive future interaction design
