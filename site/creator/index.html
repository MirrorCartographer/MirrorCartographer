THE PERSON BEHIND THE PORTALS / A CREATOR'S NOTE

HELLO.
I'M CHARITY.

I make strange little worlds for big complicated things — the questions that won't sit still, the details people overlook, and the parts of being human that refuse to fit neatly in a box.

I'm Charity Alessandra Sturgell: the person building Mirror Cartographer, collecting curious minds, making art out of the in-between, and trying to leave the internet a little more alive than I found it.

01 / WHAT I MAKE

Not a personal brand.
A whole little universe.

Mirror Cartographer is the larger project. The Living Country is the invitation into it. Inside are experiments, rooms, tools, research trails, art, sounds, and ways for people to encounter one another without having to become the same person.

01 / THE WORLDS

Places to get lost on purpose

Interactive rooms, strange residents, visual experiments, and small discoveries hiding around the next corner.

02 / THE QUESTIONS

Curiosity with its sleeves rolled up

Research and systems work that keeps sources visible, uncertainty intact, and a compelling theory from pretending it is already true.

03 / THE MAKING

Ideas that become things

Art, prototypes, instruments, writing, and collaborative experiments. Not just a mood board: things you can enter, test, use, and change.

02 / WHY IT EXISTS

“I want the internet to feel like somewhere you can actually arrive.”

Make room
for the unusual.

I’m interested in what happens when people are allowed to be complicated. When a question can stay open long enough to become useful. When creativity and rigor can share a table. When technology makes more room for human agency instead of quietly taking it away.

This project is an ongoing attempt to build that kind of place: curious, a little unruly, careful with evidence, generous with imagination, and open to revision.

— Charity
