A NOTE ABOUT THIS TOOL

This is a self-expression and reflection instrument, not a diagnostic or treatment tool. Body sensations can have physical, environmental, and other causes. You never need to explain, complete, or share a map.

Browser storage can be cleared by device settings. Export anything you want to keep elsewhere. Avoid saving sensitive details on a shared device.
