ORIGIN LENS

Begin before the name.

The recovered chronology places the precursor conversations before the product label. The product problem—helping a person express experience in their own language and examine it through more than one frame—predates the name Mirror Cartographer.

04 / EPISTEMIC BOUNDARIES

What the archive cannot tell us yet.

Missing evidence is part of the history. It should remain visible rather than being filled with a persuasive story.

Recovered, not complete

  • The conversation export covers 2024-08-22 through 2025-08-12.
  • Canvas metadata is not the same as complete revision bodies.
  • Library catalog paths are historical references, not proof that files still exist.

Designed, not necessarily running

  • A specification does not establish an implementation.
  • Source code does not establish successful deployment.
  • A deterministic test does not establish real model inference or clinical validity.

Authorship is record-specific

  • Some early concepts were user-authored; some names and formulations were assistant-generated.
  • Unknown authorship stays unknown.
  • Later summaries must not overwrite earlier source wording.

Private means private

  • Raw conversation exports, personal records, health and veterinary material, and credentials are not reproduced here.
  • Private source indexes remain in owner-restricted repositories.
  • Public counts do not grant public access to private source content.
