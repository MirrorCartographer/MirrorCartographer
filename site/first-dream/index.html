THE FIRST
DREAM

Not a personality quiz. Not a mirror that agrees. A place where sensation becomes a mark, a mark becomes a question, and a question can survive without a premature answer.

01 / MAKE A MARK

What is actually present? Give it a location, texture, shape, or name. Nothing is interpreted without your permission.

02 / HOLD THE CONTRADICTION

The mirror is not the judge.

Two statements may both matter. They do not have to be forced into agreement. Record their sources, investigate a test, or leave them unresolved.

A contradiction is not a defect. An unsupported conclusion is not evidence.

03 / THE LIVING RECORD

Locally stored, exportable, revisable. Each entry keeps observation distinct from interpretation. No invented memories, diagnosis, or server transmission.

This is a local-first research and reflection instrument, not a trained independent model, medical diagnosis, or a claim of shared memory. Entries are private to this browser unless you export them.
