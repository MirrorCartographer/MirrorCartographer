03 / RESEARCH

From signal to understanding.

Research is a process, not a claim of certainty. Every direction has a question, method, and boundary.

Evidence & claimsSource-linked claims, provenance, confidence, limits, next tests. System architecturePreserving context, state, and relationships. Methods & field notesHypotheses, experiments, evaluation, contradictions. Executable toolsValidators, demonstrations, reproducible checks, working code. A more connected societyA research argument for shared knowledge, human capability, and technology across different ways of living. Internal researcherEvidence protocol, source accountability, cross-domain research, and experimental design. Systems mapPeople, institutions, environments, signals, resources, handoffs, and feedback. Beyond the screenHuman interaction through biological signals, senses, movement, devices, and non-screen interfaces. What AI bringsPattern extraction, translation, multimodal integration, adaptation, prediction, and feedback—with limits.
EVIDENCE BOUNDARY — Visual relationships are navigational, not proof of causation. MC is not a diagnostic authority or substitute for professional care.

04 / THE CONNECTION MAP

Follow the relationships.

Biological mechanisms lead to repair questions; signals become interaction; AI supports discovery; research reaches people through social systems.

Start: the complete connection mapGuided paths through today's discussions, why links matter, and the questions they open. Degeneration → protection → regenerationAlzheimer's, MS, ALS, Parkinson's; shared mechanisms, repair and replacement. Biology ↔ technologyNeural, muscular, sensory, and biological signals as interaction channels. AI ↔ signal ↔ actionInterpreting signals, combining context, adapting feedback, and managing inference limits. Discovery ↔ societyHow research, healthcare, public systems, resources, and needs connect—or lose information. Capability ↔ a more connected societyTechnology that expands participation across different bodies, abilities, and forms of knowledge.
These are investigable relationships, not proof that connected topics share one cause or solution.
