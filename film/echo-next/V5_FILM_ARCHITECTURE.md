# V5 — Echo: film architecture

V5 preserves the architecture established in the earlier cuts, then changes the relationship between the layers.

## Preserved architecture

1. Real source footage remains the physical world.
2. Procedural scenes remain the invisible/subjective layer.
3. A custom score supplies the emotional arc.
4. The film remains text-free.
5. The visual sequence alternates observation and abstraction.

## Upgrade

The artificial layer no longer exists primarily as standalone inserts. It enters the real footage through:

- hand-authored flock glyphs
- low-opacity echo trails
- light seams integrated with existing skies
- reflections constructed from actual source frames
- constellation forms that resolve toward the bird motif

The objective is perceptual continuity: the viewer should be able to understand the invented element immediately and feel that it belongs to the photographed world.

## Sequence

`real → echo → real → threshold → real → reflection → real → constellation → return`

## Design constraint

No decorative object should exist merely because it is visually interesting. Every invented element must have a readable relationship to the birds, sky, wire, movement, reflection, or transition already present in the source footage.

## Safety / creative constraint

No synthetic person, face, identity claim, dangerous behavior, or disturbing imagery. The experiment stays within birds, sky, light, movement, reflection, sound, and abstract cinematic form.
