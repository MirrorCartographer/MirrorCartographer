# V6 Film Architecture — The World Remembers

V6 preserves the V5 architecture instead of replacing it.

## Core sequence

`observation → temporal slip → observation → flock memory → sky breath → delayed reflection → observation → clean return`

## What changed

V5 introduced invented elements into the photographed world. V6 makes the photographed world itself behave impossibly, while keeping the source footage visually dominant.

- **Temporal slip:** delayed copies of actual source frames pass through the sky as faint afterimages.
- **Sky breathing:** only the upper sky receives a very small time-shift and optical drift; the ground remains anchored.
- **Flock memory:** real bird formations are echoed from earlier moments in the same shot, so the apparition is made from the footage itself.
- **Moon/sky breath:** the photographed sky is allowed to pulse through blurred source-frame light rather than a synthetic moon or graphic rings.
- **Delayed reflection:** the lower field/water region receives a restrained reflection made from an earlier real frame, creating a second clock.
- **Clean return:** the final section removes the impossible behavior and returns attention to the unaltered world.

## Design rule

No new decorative object is introduced unless it is causally legible as a transformation of something already photographed: bird, sky, wire, light, movement, or reflection.

## Human-eye legibility

The visual language should read in this order:

1. real place
2. something is temporally wrong
3. the viewer notices the wrongness
4. the image settles back into reality

The effect should not require the viewer to understand the implementation.

## Sound architecture

The score is sparse and harmonic. Low tones establish the physical world; pulses appear only when time begins to slip; the final return reduces the musical density.

## Safety / creative constraint

No synthetic people, faces, identity claims, dangerous behavior, gore, or disturbing imagery. The invention remains abstract, environmental, and grounded in the supplied bird footage.
