# Mirror Cartographer — Complete Build Universe

This is the cross-repository construction map for the work already envisioned. It is a scope and verification contract, not a claim that every item is complete.

## Governing idea

Mirror Cartographer explores what becomes understandable when separate observations are related without losing their provenance, uncertainty, or human meaning. It must work as a research instrument, a navigable world, a personal cartography system, a public-interest systems tool, and an independent creative universe.

The experience should demonstrate its ideas rather than explain them at the visitor. The public-facing worlds should feel like places with their own laws, not dashboards wearing different colors.

## 1. Living Atlas and encounter worlds

**Purpose:** make the knowledge architecture explorable.

- The 34 curated destinations in the companion UI remain the current directory scope.
- The 79 procedural configurations in the separate Room-World catalog are not to be mislabeled as 79 finished authored rooms.
- Each destination receives its own palette, material language, geometry, motion, interaction grammar, sound identity, temporal behavior, and meaningful consequence.
- Shared code is allowed for navigation, input accessibility, persistence primitives, performance, and common lifecycle. It must not homogenize the room's renderer or rules.
- Preserve and continue: The Observatory, Machine Garden, Echo Cathedral, Carnival of Thought, The Ocean, Living Blueprint, Dream Library, Signal Forest, Impossible Museum, The Composer, Music Room, The Mind, Unreliable Observatory, Self Cartographer, Image Organism, Research Atlas, Archive, Leave a Fragment, The Weather Inside a Bell, Afterbell, Rain Organ, Afterimage Orchard, Cloud Press, Moth Dial, The Orchard That Remembers Rain, Pressure Garden, Night Shift Orchard, The House That Forgets Gravity, Connections, Veteran Mission, Veteran Hub, The World, Composition Field, and The Unmapped.
- Preserve the matte audiovisual character of the protected AV prototype; don't replace its identity with a generic glass/neon dashboard.
- Acceptance: hide the room title; the room should still be recognizable. Every meaningful control must do something observable, reversible where appropriate, and testable.

## 2. Research and evidence instrument

**Purpose:** move from fragments to defensible understanding.

- Preserve source records and provenance at the smallest useful unit.
- Distinguish observation, interpretation, claim, hypothesis, experiment, result, and decision.
- Keep uncertainty, missingness, time, measurement context, contradictory observations, and alternative explanations visible.
- Build the 101-Dimension Atlas as an expandable ontology, not a compulsory questionnaire.
- Continue The Between, the Research Atlas, the relationship engine, the Missing Evidence Engine, the Consensus Trap, Contradiction Labs, and the local administrative-failure recorder.
- Connect biomedical research and public-interest system investigations through shared methods without implying that a structural analogy proves a shared mechanism.
- Acceptance: every claim can be traced to evidence; every hypothesis has a potential falsifier; every conclusion carries its limits and unresolved gaps.

## 3. Personal cartography and continuity

**Purpose:** help a person map changing context without turning a person into a fixed label.

- Symbolic, neutral, scientific, and adaptive lenses.
- Color, shape, texture, spatial arrangement, metaphor, drawing, body/sensation mapping, and narrative fragments as optional ways to express information.
- Separate one-off private rituals from saved maps; explicit controls over what is retained or shared.
- A timeline and relationship graph for concepts, observations, decisions, evidence, symbols, and projects.
- Revisable labels, user-owned meaning, accessible nonvisual alternatives, screen-reader support, reduced-motion support, and crisis-aware boundaries.
- Acceptance: no coercive prompts, no forced interpretation, no hidden retention, no claim that a symbol is a clinical fact.

## 4. Veteran pathways and public-interest systems

**Purpose:** help people navigate fragmented institutions without pretending to be the institution.

- Veteran Hub and Veteran Mission remain distinct: the Hub is a practical service map; the Mission is a playable learning model.
- Build a Pathway Engine around: where I am, what I need, where I want to go, what constraints apply, which records are missing, what supports exist, and what happened after the last action.
- Cover housing and homelessness prevention, benefits, healthcare, VR&E and employment, education, family/caregivers, records/evidence, legal/justice pathways, crisis support, and human representation.
- Show dependencies, deadlines only when sourced, explain why a pathway is suggested, and identify when an accredited human or official agency must decide.
- Acceptance: no invented eligibility determinations, no false promises, no diagnosis, no fabricated resources, and no loss of the user's own account of events.

## 5. Creative universe

**Purpose:** make original worlds and stories that stand without needing an explanatory dashboard.

- Procedural art and Image Organism.
- Original music, Music Room, Open Mic, and sound-responsive environments.
- Animation and film language, including Mallow and other independent story worlds.
- Independent experiences such as The House That Forgets Gravity, Afterimage Orchard, Rain Organ, The Fourth Room, Signal Aquarium, and Unfinished Constellation.
- Games with distinct mechanics, progression, state, and replay value—not cosmetic reskins of one puzzle.
- Acceptance: a piece has a coherent internal law, a complete playable or watchable arc, and its own aesthetic identity.

## 6. Tools, workbenches, and learning surfaces

**Purpose:** let people inspect, remix, test, and build.

- The interactive workbench for browsing, editing, previewing, and running source artifacts.
- Field Instruments for observations, relationships, hypotheses, and research.
- Reusable validators and experiment harnesses.
- A public game/learning layer that teaches through interaction, not a wall of project explanation.
- Export/remix paths that preserve provenance and keep private data local unless the user explicitly chooses otherwise.
- Acceptance: open, interact, change state, reset, save/export where promised, and return without losing unrelated work.

## 7. Infrastructure, daemons, and release integrity

**Purpose:** keep the system maintainable and truthful.

- Free GitHub Pages is the only approved public hosting target for the two repositories.
- Preserve the two user-confirmed Vercel URLs as protected historical/canonical experiences; do not casually repoint or replace them.
- Keep private repositories private. Never publish raw conversations, sensitive personal/medical/veterinary/financial data, credentials, or private work.
- Keep the daemon distinction explicit: current repository daemons are scheduled deterministic inspectors, not model-backed autonomous coding agents.
- Expand daemons first with room-identity, provenance, route, interaction, and deployment checks. A model-backed coding worker would require an explicitly configured runtime, scoped permissions, cost controls, and review gates; do not imply one exists.
- Every handoff records repository, commit, exact changed files, tests actually run, deployment state, known defects, and next action.
- Status vocabulary: VERIFIED, CODE-ONLY, DEPLOYED-UNVERIFIED, BLOCKED.

## Construction order

1. **Integrity first:** verify all 34 destinations, correct route paths, working controls, and deployment configuration.
2. **Room identity:** finish the ten experiment renderers, then implement and test the distinct grammars for the remaining destinations.
3. **Core instruments:** connect provenance, relationship exploration, uncertainty, and the 101-Dimension Atlas into usable flows.
4. **Personal and Veteran pathways:** complete local-first continuity and explainable next-action systems.
5. **Creative completion:** make the independent worlds, games, music, and animation complete experiences.
6. **Workforce:** upgrade the deterministic daemons and establish safe reviewable coding workflows.
7. **Release:** verify each deployed route in a real browser before calling it live.

## Non-negotiable definition of done

A file existing is not a working feature. A button is not functional because it looks clickable. A design contract is not an implementation. A successful build is not a successful deployment. A plausible pattern is not evidence.

For every finished artifact, record:
- what it is for;
- what a visitor can actually do;
- what changes when they do it;
- how to reset or revisit it;
- what data persists and where;
- how accessibility and privacy are handled;
- what test was run;
- whether the live route was browser-verified.

Build the whole constellation without flattening its individual stars.
