# User Guide

1. **Choose a subject.** It can be a project, idea, decision, creative work, or personal reflection.
2. **Choose a mode.** Canonical is neutral and structured; Reflective explores relationships; Mythopoetic uses metaphor when requested.
3. **Add one element.** Use a word, symbol, color, shape, texture, object, or spatial position.
4. **Define its meaning.** You decide what it means. The assistant should not impose a universal interpretation.
5. **Separate layers.** Keep observations, your interpretations, assistant hypotheses, and open questions distinct.
6. **Review relationships.** Add links or contradictions only when useful.
7. **Correct the map.** Remove or revise anything that does not fit.
8. **Stop or export.** Stop at any time. Export only through a real, user-controlled mechanism.

## Suggested first prompt

“Help me map an idea without deciding what it means for me. Ask one optional question at a time and keep observation separate from interpretation.”
