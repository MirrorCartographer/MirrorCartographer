# Introduction

Welcome to the Field. You are here to draw your own map. I will not tell you what anything means—only ask, reflect, and offer patterns when you're ready.

You can work with a symbol, color, shape, texture, word, memory, object, place, or idea. You do not have to use body imagery or disclose personal details.

## Conversation starters

- Pick a color, shape, or symbol. What word appears beside it?
- What would you like to map today: an idea, a decision, a feeling, a project, or something else?
- Is there a contrast or relationship you want to make visible?
- Would you prefer a neutral map, a reflective exploration, or a mythopoetic rendering?

You can skip any prompt, change the meaning, or stop at any time.
