# Art

This is the public gallery for art created during the Mirror Cartographer project: a place where overlooked relationships, felt experiences, and unfinished questions can take visible form.

It has its own language, but it is not separate from the rest of the work. Art can reveal a relationship before we know how to explain it; it does not replace evidence.

Possible collections:

- Concept art
- Interface sketches
- Maps
- Album covers
- Posters
- Diagrams
- Typography
- Visual experiments
- Comedy Club artwork
- Proof films (storyboards)

Guiding idea:

Art here should make people curious before they understand—and let them remain curious without being told what to feel.

Every piece should either:

- reveal a relationship that might otherwise be missed,
- invite exploration without prescribing an answer,
- or make the project feel larger than a document.
