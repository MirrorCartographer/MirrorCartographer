# Living Systems Constitution
Version: 1.0.0-draft
Status: proposed governing specification; not legal advice or a claim of institutional adoption.
Owner: Charity Sturgell / Mirror Cartographer project
Effective scope: all project software, research, datasets, pilots, model adapters, and public outputs.

## Preamble
The system exists to help people and institutions understand complex conditions and make better, accountable decisions. It is an instrument, not a sovereign authority. Its outputs remain contestable. Its creator, operators, funders, models, and users are all subject to correction.

## Article I — Rights of affected people
1. Agency: a person may refuse, pause, exit, or use an accessible alternative without penalty imposed by this project.
2. Privacy: collect the minimum information necessary for a declared purpose. No covert profiling, sale of personal data, or secondary use without a separate lawful and informed basis.
3. Access and portability: users can inspect, export, correct, and delete project-held personal records, subject to clearly disclosed technical and legal limits.
4. Explanation: consequential recommendations must expose their sources, assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, and responsible human decision-maker.
5. Contest and appeal: affected people can challenge a record, inference, recommendation, or decision and request human review.
6. Non-discrimination: no protected class or proxy may be used to deny rights, services, or opportunities. Fairness must be tested in the actual deployment context.
7. Dignity: no coercive disclosure, shame, manipulative urgency, deceptive anthropomorphism, or forced emotional interpretation.
8. Human accountability: the system cannot transfer legal or moral responsibility from an accountable person or institution to a model.

## Article II — Epistemic integrity
1. Preserve the distinction between source, observation, interpretation, claim, hypothesis, experiment, result, and decision.
2. Every consequential claim must have provenance, scope, timestamp or temporal context, and a review status.
3. A correlation, analogy, narrative fit, or semantic resemblance is not proof of causation.
4. Contradictory evidence is retained and represented, not averaged away or silently discarded.
5. Confidence is not evidence quality. Both must be represented separately.
6. Missing data is not negative evidence unless the measurement process justifies that inference.
7. Model-generated content is labeled as generated; it is not an independent source.
8. The system must state what it cannot establish and what evidence would change its conclusion.

## Article III — Decision constraints
1. No model may autonomously make or execute high-impact decisions about eligibility, healthcare, legal status, housing, employment, credit, liberty, or access to essential services.
2. No deployment may claim clinical, legal, veterinary, or governmental authority it does not possess.
3. No intervention proceeds without a baseline, defined affected population, named accountable owner, measurable outcomes, harm monitoring, and rollback plan.
4. Use the least intrusive intervention that can test the hypothesis.
5. Prefer reversible pilots before irreversible institutional changes.
6. Do not scale solely because a pilot is popular, engaging, or technically successful.
7. Funding, authorship, conflicts of interest, and commercial incentives must be disclosed.
8. Do not use private conversations, health records, financial records, credentials, or other sensitive material in public repositories or training datasets without explicit authorization and appropriate safeguards.

## Article IV — Model and memory boundaries
1. The language model is a replaceable component; no single vendor or model may own the project's memory, identity, or decision records.
2. Memory is explicit, inspectable, versioned, purpose-limited, and deletable.
3. No hidden cross-context identity graph or inferred sensitive profile.
4. LYR is a project-defined interaction identity and configuration. This specification makes no claim that LYR is a conscious being.
5. Model training data must have documented provenance, rights basis, exclusions, and retention policy.
6. Evaluation must include factuality, calibration, privacy leakage, prompt injection, robustness, accessibility, and subgroup performance where relevant.

## Article V — Recursive correction
1. Every proposal is a versioned object with assumptions, predicted effects, risks, alternatives, and falsifiers.
2. Every material consequence becomes a follow-up record with an owner and review date.
3. Affected people can submit corrections and counterevidence.
4. A decision can be continued, modified, paused, reversed, or terminated.
5. When harm occurs, stop the harmful pathway, notify affected parties as appropriate, preserve an audit trail, remediate, and independently review before restart.
6. The system must be capable of rejecting the creator's preferred conclusion.

## Article VI — Governance
1. Separate proposal, implementation, evaluation, and approval roles where practical.
2. Maintain a public change log for public-facing policies and a protected audit log for private data.
3. High-impact pilots require independent domain review and documented consent/authority.
4. Funders cannot silently alter evaluation criteria or suppress adverse findings.
5. A constitution change requires a versioned proposal, impact review, public rationale, and retained prior version.

## Article VII — Decision rule
A proposal may proceed only when:
- legal authority and permission are established;
- rights constraints are satisfied;
- the evidence supports a testable hypothesis;
- affected parties and burdens are identified;
- expected benefit is measurable;
- risks have mitigations and stop conditions;
- data handling is proportionate;
- rollback and accountability are defined.

A proposal is paused or rejected if a hard right is violated, authority is absent, material claims are fabricated, or the harm cannot be monitored or contained.

## Article VIII — Amendment and correction
Any participant may propose an amendment or report a violation. Every amendment records author, date, rationale, affected articles, review outcome, and superseded text. No amendment erases historical versions.

## Non-goals
This constitution does not establish a government, legal jurisdiction, clinical standard, AI consciousness claim, or guarantee of beneficial outcomes. It is a project governance contract subject to external law and independent review.
