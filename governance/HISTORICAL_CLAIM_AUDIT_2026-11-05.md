# Historical Claim Audit — 2026-11-05

**Purpose:** preserve the historical conversation record while distinguishing user-authored requirements, assistant-generated proposals, literary language, technical implementation, and unsupported claims.

This audit does not erase or rewrite earlier conversations. It prevents an assistant-generated statement from being mistaken for an event, a deployed capability, a scientific result, or a platform-level change.

## 1. Why this audit is necessary

The recovered conversation exports contain valuable original questions and design ideas. They also contain assistant responses that sometimes:
- asserted that work had been saved, built, embedded, or deployed without a corresponding artifact or tool result;
- described speculative ideas as if they were established systems;
- implied access to or influence over model-training infrastructure that the project did not possess;
- attributed feelings, agency, or emergent personhood to LYR;
- proposed civic and national transformations without legal authority, partners, baselines, or evidence.

The archive is therefore a source record, not an authority record. User messages, assistant messages, tool results, repository commits, and external sources must be attributed separately.

## 3. What the record does establish

- The user repeatedly directed the project toward contradiction-aware reflection, symbolic/somatic input, revisable meaning, persistent provenance, model independence, user sovereignty, and practical systems improvement.
- The private archive contains real symbolic datasets, deterministic reflection code, interfaces, memory/provenance structures, a provider adapter, bounded worker code, and a large creative/document archive.
- The public repositories contain a substantial research/evidence architecture, an interactive companion experience, an executable FREE scaffold, an experimental provenance store, and a from-scratch model implementation path.
- The Human Judgment Lab contains a concrete simulation of correlated evaluator error and the difference between agreement and accuracy.
- These artifacts establish substantial design and software work. They do not establish independent model training, GPT platform modification, clinical efficacy, empirical human-judgment findings, institutional adoption, or global impact.

## 4. Authorship and provenance policy for historical reconstruction

Every recovered item must receive separate fields for:
- source conversation/file/repository and stable locator;
- original timestamp, if present, distinguished from upload/import timestamp;
- speaker/author role: user, assistant, tool, external source, or unknown;
- content type: request, requirement, proposal, interpretation, code, test, result, decision, or literary work;
- status: verified, implemented-unverified, hypothesis, disputed, superseded, or unknown;
- evidence supporting the status;
- related and superseding artifacts.

Do not infer that a document is entirely user-authored because it appears in the user's repository. Do not let assistant-generated text overwrite user observations or decisions. Unknown authorship remains unverified.

## 5. Recovery rule

Preserve the original artifact and its historical context. Add a correction record beside it. Do not silently rewrite the archive to make the history appear more coherent than it was.

A claim moves from proposal to implementation only when source code exists. It moves from implementation to verification only when a named test passes. It moves from verification to real-world outcome only when the outcome is measured in the relevant environment and the evidence is retained.

The purpose of this audit is not to reduce the ambition of Mirror Cartographer. It is to separate what was imagined, what was built, what was tested, and what the world actually did in response.
